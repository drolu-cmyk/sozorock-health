New York meets the smallest share of primary-care need in its region.

In shortage areas, New York has about a third of the primary-care physicians needed. New Jersey and Vermont have more than four-fifths.

Percent of need met compares available primary-care physicians with the number needed to remove a shortage designation. It is not the share of people receiving care. Same-date snapshot; not a trend.

One clear route to a doctor who knows you.

The Primary Care Access Program sponsors a direct primary care membership with Dr. Michael Purcell and Purcell Integrative Osteopathic Care in Clifton Park, plus the support that makes care usable.

Clinical care stays with the licensed provider. Membership is not health insurance. Part of the SozoRock Health Equity Initiative.
