# Care for every ZIP Code — treatment (review cut v2)

Length 47 s · 1920×1080 · 30 fps · SozoRock Health palette (navy #071D3B, cobalt #0644AD, icy #EFF5FF, orange highlight #F28A3C) · Inter Display.

## Structures considered
1. **Scale journey (chosen):** one dot stands for people. 5.29 million dots' worth of need becomes a map of New York, then a ranking, then a single person, then 25 people, then the whole state again. It turns statistics into one human and back, and ends where it began.
2. Before/after split screen (today's barriers vs the program). Rejected: no outcome data yet, so an "after" would overclaim.
3. Countdown to January 2027. Rejected: date-driven but empty of evidence.

## Logline and arc
Millions of New Yorkers live where primary care is scarce. One year of sponsored care for up to 25 people is where SozoRock starts to change that.
Setup (scale of the shortage) → turn (one dot: "What if care came within reach?") → ending (25 lit dots inside the same field; "Care for every ZIP Code.").

## Benchmark (grammar only)
Learn: one dot as a person, one idea per screen, the chart as the camera, an echoing ending. Do not take: any existing chart layouts, typefaces, music or shots.

## Honesty rules
Every number on screen comes from HRSA FY2026 Q4 Table 3 (as of September 30, 2026) or CDC PLACES 2025 release county estimates (2023 data). The 1.8 million figure is a SozoRock population-weighted calculation from the county estimates and is labelled as an estimate. Clinical care stays with the licensed provider; membership is not insurance. No outcome claims.
