# CB-CAP product contract

CB-CAP means County-Based Community Access Platform. Its market-facing descriptor is **County planning for health access**. Its promise is **From evidence to a plan you can defend.**

This contract replaces the earlier stakeholder-preview positioning following the product brief of September 7, 2026. Public copy is centralized in `apps/platform/app/product-contract.json`. Verified capability states and dependencies are recorded in `cbcap-capability-status.json`; implementation never establishes production availability by itself.

## Three distinct experiences

Place Intelligence provides open public evidence exploration. CB-CAP's Public Evidence Preview demonstrates county-planning value using a real county. The institutional Planning Workspace brings authorized evidence into planning, resources, review, monitoring and decision records. Deep public exploration belongs in Place Intelligence.

## County planning

See the county picture. Turn evidence into a plan. Line up the resources. Keep the plan current. Sources stay visible. Assumptions stay labeled. People decide.

Public navigation: Product, For Organizations, Methods, Sign in. Institutional navigation: Overview, Evidence, Plan, Resources, Monitor, Governance. AI is infrastructure, not a primary navigation category.

Funding Fit checks reviewed requirements against evidence and identifies missing requirements, conflicts and unknowns. It does not determine final eligibility or award probability. Budget & Funding uses reviewed fiscal evidence separately from Funding Fit. Workforce & Capacity requires supporting sources. Test Options illustrates assumptions rather than predicting demand. What Changed prompts another review. Decision Record preserves reviewed decisions and supersession.

## Evidence discipline

Use Source estimate, Planning View, User assumption, Planning scenario, Local Evidence, Planned source and Not available. Missing values remain missing, never zero. County, city and ZIP-linked evidence remain distinct. Preserve geography, vintage, denominator, uncertainty and exact sources. Differences do not establish statistical significance.

A composite Planning View is optional. Show components, formula, weights, coverage, missingness and limitations. It is not a government designation, county grade, funding formula, causal model, clinical-risk score or validated county ranking. Default maps to published evidence or coverage.

## Methods and publication relationship

*Rethinking Rural Governance* is the conceptual foundation for Evidence -> Interpretation -> Lever, integration, fiscal and workforce context, planning, governance and continuous learning. It is not a source for current health estimates, evidence of statistical validation, or permission to publish historical maturity charts as current county scores.

The maturity model remains Data Capture -> Operational Execution -> Structured Integration -> Systems Intelligence -> Institutional Intelligence. Explain it under Methods and describe release status truthfully.

## Human authority and confidentiality

AI drafts. People decide. CB-CAP does not diagnose, triage, prescribe, allocate funding, determine final eligibility, set county priorities or approve its own output. It does not replace county judgment or CHA/CHIP governance. The public preview contains no resident records or PHI. Authorized institutional claims require reviewed documents, versions, geographic scope, citation locators, reviewer identity and supersession status. Missing local evidence means Not found in reviewed evidence, not that a county does not perform an activity.

Production release requires exact-main and CI evidence, protected deployment, domain and API checks, responsive and accessibility QA, verified capability flags, institutional login, tenant isolation and exact-run review. The public preview must remain available when institutional services are unavailable.
