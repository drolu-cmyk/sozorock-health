# SozoRock Place Intelligence — Fall 2026 release record

This record describes the Explore-only release work. It is an engineering and operating record, not a traction claim or a funding application.

## Product boundary

Place Intelligence is a county-first, evidence-grounded planning surface inside `/explore`. ZIP Codes, ZCTAs and cities are search inputs; the selected Census county or county equivalent is the evidence scope. The marketing site, homepage, CB-CAP product and mobile application are outside this release.

## Release capabilities

- Every primary-scope Census county and county equivalent is represented by the approved geography snapshot and can return the versioned place brief contract.
- The Evidence Core keeps source release, data period, geography, direction, uncertainty, freshness and citation metadata together.
- The Place Agent is limited to approved read-only evidence tools and fails closed for unsupported, clinical or individual-risk requests.
- County workspaces support authenticated participants, append-only events, scoped share links, one-time handoffs, versioned forks, scenarios and funder snapshots.
- Pilot onboarding is self-serve and review-gated. A submitted form is a request for review, not enrollment or proof of adoption.
- Source maintenance detects candidate changes and opens guarded review work; no release replaces an approved snapshot silently.
- Usage, latency, failure and correction signals are privacy-safe operational measures. Test and staging activity is excluded from traction metrics.

## Evidence standard

The release does not infer local priorities from national modeled measures. A local CHA, CHIP, CHNA or CSP is shown with its actual document scope and review state. Unverified, stale, unavailable or incompatible evidence remains labeled as such.

## Pilot and traction statement

No customer, pilot, partner, usage, retention or funding result is claimed here. The repository contains test fixtures and controlled staging checks. Production traction may be reported only from production onboarding records after human review and only with an auditable source and date.

## Demo status

The YC demo storyboard and capture checklist are maintained separately. A final 60-second public demo requires approved product footage, approved narration and a human review of every claim before publication. Until that review is recorded, no video is described as a verified customer or traction demonstration.
