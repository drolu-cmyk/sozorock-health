# Entity and intellectual-property readiness checklist

This checklist records work required before external commercial or institutional commitments. It intentionally does not claim that an item is complete.

| Area | Status | Owner | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legal entity and authority to operate | in_review | Foundation counsel | Current organizational records and authorized signatory |
| SozoRock Health trademark usage | in_review | Foundation counsel | Clearance, ownership and licensing record |
| Publication and video copyrights | in_review | Foundation counsel | Asset provenance, permissions and creator agreements |
| Census/CDC/ACS/HRSA/AHRQ data licenses | in_review | Evidence owner | Source terms, release identifiers and retained artifacts |
| Privacy notice and retention schedule | in_review | Privacy owner | Approved notice, consent language and deletion procedure |
| County partner and provider agreements | not_started | Partnerships owner | Approved templates and scope-of-practice boundaries |

The application must not state “patented,” “exclusive,” “approved partner,” or a completed funding relationship unless the underlying evidence is attached and reviewed.
